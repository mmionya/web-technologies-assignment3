A few liner notes.

New to the music? Here’s a place to start.

✳

Try Constant Companions, then explore the earlier albums above. There’s no required order: choose a cover that catches your eye and press play on Bandcamp.

Singing-synthesis software lets musicians shape sung notes and words using voicebanks. The album credits list Synthesizer V and VOCALOID6, including voices such as Kasane Teto, GUMI, and ANRI.
